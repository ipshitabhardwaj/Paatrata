Benefit
"Financial assistance of Rs. 1000/- per month in the form of pension" to the widow of a male
registered construction worker of the Board.

Eligibility (paraphrased)
1. The deceased worker must have been a member of the Board for at least the last six months.
2. The widow must apply within one year of the worker's death.
3. A death certificate from the competent authority is required, along with a self-declaration that
   she does not receive pension from any other source.
4. "The financial assistance will not be given to those beneficiaries who are covered under EPF."
