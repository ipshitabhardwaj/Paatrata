Eligibility
1. Children in the age group of 0-6 years.
2. Pregnant women.
3. Lactating mothers.
4. Women from age group 14-18 years of UT Chandigarh.
5. The Scheme is universal for all categories of beneficiaries and in coverage.

Benefits
Supplementary Nutrition; Immunization; Health Check-up; Referral Services; Pre-School Education;
Nutrition & Health Education. Services are delivered at Anganwadi Centres.

Application
The citizen need not apply to the scheme. Visit the nearest Anganwadi center.
