Eligibility
1. The applicant women's age should be 18-60 years.
2. The applicant should be a resident of UT, Chandigarh for more than 3 years.
3. The applicant's family's annual income should not exceed Rs. 1,50,000/-

Benefit
Pension Amount To Widows And Destitute Women - Rs. 1,000/- per month.

Documents required
Residence proof of three years (Voter Card, Ration Card, Electricity Bill etc).
Death certificate of husband (In case of widow).
Aadhar Card. Mobile Number.
