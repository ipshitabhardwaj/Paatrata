Eligibility
1. The family annual income of the applicant should not exceed Rs. 1,50,000.
2. The applicant should be 60 years of age or above.
3. The applicant should have been residing in Chandigarh UT for more than 3 years.
4. Only applicants from Chandigarh are eligible.

Benefit
A pension of Rs. 1,000 per month.

Documents required
Two passport-size photographs; proof of residence for three years (voter card, ration card or
electricity bill); Aadhaar card; mobile number.
